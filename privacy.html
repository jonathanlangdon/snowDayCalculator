Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Location and weather data

If you choose “Get your location,” your browser asks for permission to access your latitude and longitude. Your coordinates are sent directly from your browser to the National Weather Service at weather.gov to retrieve a local forecast and active weather alerts.

If you check the option to save your location, the coordinates are also stored in your browser's local storage. They remain on your device until you clear your browser data. The site does not save your coordinates on its server as part of the snow-day calculation.

Advertising

Once advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads to you.

You can manage or opt out of personalized Google advertising in My Ad Center. You can also learn about choices offered by participating advertising companies at YourAdChoices.

Your choices

You may decline the browser's location request, leave the save-location box unchecked, clear this site's local storage, and adjust cookie or ad personalization settings in your browser and Google account.
